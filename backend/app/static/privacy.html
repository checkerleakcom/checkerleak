ARTICLE 1 Core Operating Philosophy: Utility as a Service

Pure Infrastructure Provider

CheckerLeak.com is engineered exclusively as a high-throughput, technical verification engine. Our relationship with our users is that of a pure utility service provider, analogous to a telecom switch or a DNS resolver.

We do not operate as a data broker, marketing list aggregator, or leak intelligence repository. ARTICLE 2 Ephemeral RAM Execution Architecture

How Your Verification Queries Are Processed

When you submit a phone number or email for verification via our Web Portal or REST API:

  1. Encrypted Ingress: Your request arrives over an encrypted TLS 1.3 channel.
  2. Volatile Memory Ingestion: The payload is held solely in volatile Random Access Memory (RAM) within our backend runtime.
  3. Protocol Signaling: The selected engine (e.g. WhatsApp status probe, Apple iMessage APNs ping, HLR carrier routing) performs the real-time handshake.
  4. Client Response Delivery: The structured JSON verification response is transmitted directly back to your client session.
  5. Immediate Memory Purge: The queried phone number and verification payload are instantly dereferenced and purged by memory garbage collection. No trace is committed to disk, relational databases, persistent caching, or log aggregators.
Technical Guarantee:
Target Persistence Duration: < 150 milliseconds (RAM only). Disk Persistence: 0.0000%

ARTICLE 4 Information We Legitimate Retain for Account Management

What We Do Store (Purely Operational)

To maintain your account, authenticate API calls, and credit your balance, we store only the minimal operational data required:

  • User Account Data: Your registration email address and salted bcrypt password hash (or Google OAuth identifier).
  • API Credentials: Your unique alphanumeric API key for request authentication.
  • Billing Ledger: Blockchain transaction hashes (BTC, USDT, ETH, SOL) and credit balance count to prove deposit settlement. We do not store credit card numbers.
  • Aggregated Platform Telemetry: Anonymized platform performance counters (e.g. total volume of WhatsApp queries run across all users today, average response latency in milliseconds, engine uptime rates).
