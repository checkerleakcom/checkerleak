Privacy Policy

Last modified: October 2026

At CheckerLeak (accessible from checkerleak.com), we provide programmatic verification services and developer APIs. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we process, how that information is handled, and the principles governing our infrastructure.

By accessing CheckerLeak, using our API endpoints, or registering an account, you agree to the collection and handling of information in accordance with this document.

1. Verification Requests and Ephemeral Processing

CheckerLeak provides technical validation tools that inspect phone numbers, email addresses, and public network protocols.

When you execute a verification request through our web interface or REST API:

  • Real-Time Execution: Queries are processed in temporary volatile memory (RAM) to communicate with the relevant network nodes and assemble the response.
  • No Retention of Targets: The query targets (such as phone numbers, emails, or usernames) submitted to our endpoints are not written to persistent databases, log aggregators, or permanent disk storage.
  • Purge upon Delivery: Once the response payload is returned to your client session, the operational variables in memory are cleared. We do not maintain searchable histories or secondary directories of targets queried by our users.

2. Information We Collect for Account Operations

To manage user accounts, authenticate API requests, and process subscription credits, we collect the minimum information required for regular platform operation:

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your email address and an encrypted hash of your password. If you authenticate via third-party providers (such as Google), we receive your basic profile identifier as permitted by your provider settings.
  • API Credentials: Each registered account receives an API token used to authenticate calls made to our services.
  • Billing Records: For cryptocurrency deposits (such as BTC, USDT, ETH, or SOL), we record the associated public blockchain transaction hash and the resulting balance credit. We do not collect or store credit card details or bank account credentials.

3. Usage Metrics and Technical Logs

Like most web services, our web servers collect standard technical information automatically to maintain security, optimize routing, and protect against abusive traffic:

  • Browser user-agent, operating system category, and approximate geographic region (country level).
  • HTTP response codes, response latency, and aggregated endpoint volume counters.
  • IP addresses may be temporarily evaluated by firewall and DDoS protection systems (such as Cloudflare) to prevent brute-force attacks and rate-limit violations, after which they are handled according to standard transit retention rules.

4. Data Security

We employ industry-standard administrative and technical safeguards to secure our infrastructure and your account credentials:

  • All communication between your client and our services is encrypted in transit via modern Transport Layer Security (TLS).
  • User passwords are hashed using salt-based cryptographic functions (bcrypt) and are never stored in plaintext.
  • Administrative access to backend infrastructure is restricted via multi-factor authentication and strict access controls.

5. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

CheckerLeak does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, account data, or inquiry records to marketers, advertising brokers, or data resellers.

Information is shared only under the following standard operational circumstances:

  • Infrastructure Providers: We rely on reputable cloud hosting, network edge providers, and upstream telecommunication networks to deliver network routing and high availability.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose account details if formally required to comply with a valid legal process, applicable law, or enforceable government order. Because verification targets are not retained on disk, our response to data requests is naturally limited to account registration and billing records.

6. Your Rights and Data Management

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the European Economic Area under GDPR and the state of California under CCPA), you may have specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access and Portability: You may request a summary of the account information we hold associated with your profile.
  • Correction: You may update your account settings and credentials directly through the client dashboard.
  • Deletion: You may request the permanent deletion of your account and associated billing records by contacting our support desk.

7. Cookies

CheckerLeak uses minimal first-party session cookies and browser local storage strictly necessary for authentication state and user preferences (such as maintaining your login session across page views). We do not use intrusive third-party cross-site advertising trackers.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our infrastructure, service offerings, or legal obligations. The date of the latest revision will always be displayed at the top of this document. Continued use of CheckerLeak after revisions constitute your acceptance of the updated terms.
